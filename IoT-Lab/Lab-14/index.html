1. Why do we analyze IoT data?

Imagine a smart classroom with a sensor measuring temperature and humidity. One reading tells us the current condition. Many readings help us find patterns and identify problems.

IoT Sensor→Readings→Data Analysis→Decision / Alert

Temperature (°C)

How hot or cold the room is.

Humidity (%)

How much moisture is in the air.

Average

Sum of readings ÷ number of readings.

Threshold

A chosen limit used to identify unusual readings.

Important: This is a simulated dataset. The browser does not read a real sensor. A temperature above the lab threshold is a practice alert, not a medical or safety warning.

3. How do we analyze the readings?

Average

Add all temperature readings and divide by the number of readings.

Example: (24 + 26 + 28) ÷ 3 = 26°C

Highest and lowest

Find the largest and smallest temperature values in the dataset.

Alert rule

If temperature is greater than 30°C, display HIGH. Otherwise, display NORMAL.

Try a prediction

Before changing a temperature, guess what will happen to the average and alert count. Then change a value in the dashboard and check your answer.
