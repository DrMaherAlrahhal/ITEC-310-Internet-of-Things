1 · What makes Industrial IoT different?

Ordinary IoT

Often focuses on convenience, home automation and personal monitoring.

Smart lampHome thermostat

Industrial IoT (IIoT)

Connects machines, sensors and operational systems where reliability, safety and downtime matter.

Factory motorWater plant
Typical industrial loop:
Sense — machine temperature, vibration, flow or pressure
Communicate — industrial network or gateway
Analyze — local rules, trends or cloud analytics
Act — alert an operator or execute approved control logic
Verify — measure the outcome and record evidence

6 · Operational challenges

Reliability

Network or sensor failures can hide dangerous conditions. Design fallback behavior and monitor device health.

Cybersecurity

Use access control, protected communication, segmentation and controlled updates.

Legacy equipment

Older machines may require compatible interfaces or carefully designed gateways.

Business value

Measure outcomes such as downtime, energy use, defects and maintenance costs—not only sensor count.
