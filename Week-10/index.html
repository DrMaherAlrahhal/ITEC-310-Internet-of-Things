1. Cloud, fog and edge: three processing locations

📍 EdgeInside or next to the device. Fast local decisions; limited compute and storage.
🏢 FogA nearby gateway or local server. Coordinates devices, filters data and supports low-latency services.
☁️ CloudRemote data center. Scalable storage, cross-site analysis and large workloads; depends on connectivity for remote access.

Week 10 takeaway

Edge supports immediate device-level decisions. Fog coordinates nearby devices and reduces upstream traffic. Cloud supports scalable storage and large-scale analytics. Strong IoT designs often combine all three.
